How it works

From "anyone free this week?"
to confirmed plans in minutes.

1

Create an event

Name it, add a few time slots, invite your friends. Takes about 30 seconds.

2

Everyone votes

Friends vote on what works. No app required. They can respond from a link in their browser.

3

Confirm and go

You pick the winner. Everyone gets a push notification. Plans made.

Features

Everything your group chat
can't do.

📅

Real availability, not guesswork

Connect Google Calendar and see when your friends are actually free. No more "I think I'm free Saturday?"

🗳️

Group time voting

Propose multiple slots and let everyone vote. Results are live, so you'll know within hours, not days.

📍

Venue voting with a map

Suggest places, see them on a live map, vote on your favourite. No more "idk, you pick."

📸

Photo Moments

At a random moment during your event, gather asks everyone to take a photo at the same time. A memory captured, not staged.

🔔

Smart notifications

Get notified when friends vote, respond, or when your event is confirmed. Tap any notification to jump straight there.

👥

Private by design

gather is for your actual friend groups: sports teams, uni mates, work friends. Not strangers, not algorithms.
