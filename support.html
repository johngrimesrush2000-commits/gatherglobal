Common questions

How do I add friends?

Go to the Friends tab and search by @username. Send a friend request and once accepted, you can start planning events together.

How do I connect Google Calendar?

Go to Profile → Integrations → Google Calendar and tap Connect. Your availability will then show when friends invite you to events.

How do I turn off notifications?

Go to Profile → Settings → Notifications and adjust your preferences there.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all associated data.

How do I reset my password?

On the login screen tap Forgot password, enter your email, and we'll send you a reset link.
